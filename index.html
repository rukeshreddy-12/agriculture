About Agriculture

Importance of Agriculture

Agriculture is the process of growing crops and raising animals for food and other useful products.

It plays an important role in the Indian economy by providing food, employment and raw materials for industries.

Modern agriculture uses technology, machinery, irrigation and scientific methods to improve farming practices.

Fertilizer Information

Fertilizers provide nutrients required for plant growth.

🌱 Organic Fertilizers

Examples: Compost, farmyard manure and vermicompost.

🧪 Nitrogen Fertilizers

Help plants develop healthy leaves and vegetative growth.

🌿 Phosphorus Fertilizers

Support root development and plant growth.

🍀 Potassium Fertilizers

Support plant health and help plants manage environmental stress.

Irrigation Methods

Different ways to supply water to agricultural crops.

Drip Irrigation

Water is supplied near plant roots through pipes and emitters.

Sprinkler Irrigation

Water is distributed over crops in the form of artificial rainfall.

Surface Irrigation

Water flows over the soil surface to reach crop roots.
